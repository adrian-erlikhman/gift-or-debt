    Add an offer

    Copy the text of the letter or the aid portal page and paste it here. A PDF works too. Fall/spring columns, per-semester amounts and footnotes are handled.

    Use Claude (optional, your own key)

    Claude can read photos, scanned PDFs and odd layouts, and reword the email to the aid office. It never does the math: every amount it reads is checked against the letter's text before the page uses it. Your key stays in this tab and is sent only to api.anthropic.com.

    How it works

    1. Read the letter

    Rules find every dollar amount and what it belongs to: cost, grant, loan or work-study. Totals are set aside and checked against the lines. Fall/spring columns and “per semester” amounts are turned into a year. Every line keeps the words it came from, and you can fix any of them.

    2. Do the math in the open

    Net price is the cost of attendance minus gift aid. Loans and work-study don't lower it; they're ways to pay it. Four years: costs grow by an assumed rate you can change, first-year-only awards drop off, and borrowing repeats. Interest builds on unsubsidized, private and Parent PLUS loans while you're in school.

    3. Flag the tricks

    Loans counted as aid. A Parent PLUS loan quietly closing the gap. Books and transportation left out. A scholarship that's first-year only. Totals that don't match their lines. Then it drafts polite questions for the aid office.

    Loan terms are for loans first disbursed July 1, 2026 to June 30, 2027 (Federal Student Aid, GENERAL-26-33): 6.52% for undergraduate Direct Loans with a 1.057% fee, 9.07% for Parent PLUS with a 4.228% fee. Monthly payments assume the standard 10-year plan. These are estimates to check with the school, not financial advice.
